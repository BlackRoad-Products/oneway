01 — Features
Forward-Only. Tamper-Proof.
Every export carries cryptographic proof that your data left intact and unaltered. No trust required.
M

Cryptographic Manifests

Every export generates a SHA-256 manifest listing every file, its hash, and its lineage. If a single byte changed, the manifest breaks. You get a tamper-evident receipt for every piece of data that leaves the system.

P

Proof Certificates

Each export includes a signed proof certificate anchored to RoadChain. Third parties can independently verify that your data was exported at a specific time, from a specific state, without contacting BlackRoad at all.

S

Scheduled Exports

Set it and forget it. Configure recurring exports — daily, weekly, or on every state change. Your data streams out on your schedule to your destination. S3, local disk, email, webhook — wherever you want it.

F

Forward-Only Verification

OneWay exports are append-only and forward-only. Once data leaves, the export log is immutable. No rewriting history. No silent deletions. The chain of custody is permanent and publicly auditable.
